## English

**In short.** Luma Valley is an offline game. It has no account, ads, ad tracking, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and the game sends no personal data to us or to any other server.

**What is stored on your device.** Your game progress (board, inventory, quests, coins and tokens), your settings (language, sound, vibration, motion) and the last open tab are stored only on this device, in the app's own storage, so you can continue where you left off. None of it reaches us.

**Deleting it.** Settings → Save → "Reset progress" deletes the game progress on this device; permanent items bought with real money are kept. Deleting the app removes all game data from the device. There is no cloud backup or sync between devices.

**Purchases.** When real-money purchases are enabled, payments are handled only by the App Store or Google Play under their privacy policies. We never see card or payment details. The game keeps only the store's transaction id on the device, so the same item is never granted twice.

**Stores and the operating system.** The store you downloaded the app from (Apple, Google) and your device's operating system may process download, update and crash information under their own terms. This is outside our control and does not reach us as personal data.

**Children.** The game collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

**Your rights (GDPR, KVKK).** Because we process no personal data about you, we hold no record to access, correct or erase. You can delete the data on your device as described above. Questions can go to the support address.

**Changes.** If a feature that processes data is ever added, this text is updated before that feature ships and the date above changes.
